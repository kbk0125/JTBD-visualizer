# Job-map method

Use the eight universal stages described by Strategyn's customer-centered innovation map:

| Stage | Customer intent | Useful drafting prompt |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Define | Determine goals and plan resources. | What must the executor decide before starting? |
| Locate | Gather needed information and items. | What inputs must be found or accessed? |
| Prepare | Set up the environment and inputs. | What must be arranged or made ready? |
| Confirm | Verify readiness. | What must be checked before execution begins? |
| Execute | Carry out the core job. | What central transformation or progress occurs? |
| Monitor | Assess whether execution is succeeding. | What signals show progress, quality, or risk? |
| Modify | Adjust to improve execution. | What changes when progress is off track? |
| Conclude | Finish or prepare to repeat. | What must be closed, saved, handed off, or reset? |

Source: [Strategyn — The Jobs-To-Be-Done Framework](https://strategyn.com/jobs-to-be-done/customer-centered-innovation-map/)

## Quality checks

A valid job step:

- states what the customer is trying to accomplish rather than the feature they use;
- applies across plausible solutions and is not unique to one vendor's flow;
- belongs in only one of the eight stages;
- begins with a clear verb and has an observable result;
- is supported by evidence or visibly marked as an inference.

Define Execute first to establish the map's frame. Then map Define through Confirm and finally Monitor through Conclude. If an activity is really a more fundamental goal, move up a level; if it describes a button or screen, rewrite it as the underlying intent.
